The seven-day build period begins once the deposit has cleared and the items below have been received. The more of this you can send in one go, the sooner your website goes live.

Anything not yet available will be given a professional placeholder and replaced later at no additional cost — so please don't hold up the whole list waiting on one item.

1. Logo and Branding Priority

  • Logo file — ideally a PNG with a transparent background or an SVG. A high-resolution JPG is also fine.
  • If you have a version of the logo designed for dark backgrounds (white or light), please send that as well.
  • Confirm the brand colours are correct: navy blue, green and gold, as taken from your flyer.
No logo yet?

Not a problem. A placeholder emblem will be used and replaced free of charge the moment the artwork exists.

2. Team Photographs and Details

For each person who will appear on the About page:

  • Full name
  • Role or job title (for example, Director, or Lead Mathematics Tutor)
  • Photograph — clear, well lit, head and shoulders, facing the camera. A good phone photo is perfectly acceptable; please avoid heavily filtered images or faces cropped out of group shots.
  • Optional: one or two lines about them — qualifications, years of experience, subjects taught
  • Confirm how many people will be featured, so the layout can be designed correctly. Four to six works best visually.

3. Testimonials

Your flyer carries a single quote. A dedicated testimonials page needs more than that to look credible.

  • Four to six genuine testimonials from parents or students. For each one, please provide:
    • The quote itself
    • Who it is from — a first name is sufficient (for example, “Mrs. A., parent of a Year 10 student” or “Year 11 Student”)
    • A star rating out of five, if you record them
Consent

Please ensure you have permission to publish each testimonial, particularly where the student is under eighteen.

4. Enquiry Email Address

  • Which inbox should website enquiries be delivered to? (for example, info@testportalva.org)
  • Should a copy also be sent to a second address as a backup?

5. Website Text

Draft wording will be written for you based on your flyer, which you will review and correct during the revision round. What would help now:

  • A short paragraph on how the Academy started and what makes it different — three to five sentences is plenty.
  • Confirm the tutor credentials you would like published — for example, DBS-checked, qualified teachers, subject specialists.
  • Confirm how lessons are delivered (Microsoft Teams, per your flyer) and whether any other platform is used.

6. Claims to Verify

The following appear on your flyer and will be published prominently on the website. Once published they become public claims, so please confirm each is current and can be substantiated.

  • 300+ students have trusted the Academy
  • 4.9 out of 5 parent rating
  • 95% GCSE pass rate
  • Curricula followed: British National Curriculum, US Common Core, Australian, Canadian Provincial
  • Subjects: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Computer Science, English Language — all Year 7 to A-Level
  • Examination boards: AQA, Pearson Edexcel, OCR

7. Contact Details

Please confirm these are still correct:

  • Phone and WhatsApp: +44 73950 16174
  • Email: info@testportalva.org
  • Address: 135 Great Horton Road, Bradford, BD7 1AL, United Kingdom
  • Any social media accounts to link to — Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube

8. Pricing

  • Would you like lesson prices shown on the website, or should enquirers be asked to contact you for a quotation?

Either approach works. Publishing prices reduces time-wasting enquiries; withholding them generates more enquiries, though a greater share will be casual.

9. Questions for the FAQ Section

The contact page will carry a short list of frequently asked questions. Please answer whichever apply:

  • How long is a typical lesson, and how often do students attend?
  • What is the class size for small-group sessions?
  • Is the free trial lesson genuinely free, and how does a parent book it?
  • What is the cancellation or rescheduling policy?
  • How and when do parents receive progress reports?
  • Are all tutors DBS-checked?

Sending the Files

Please send everything in one batch where possible — WhatsApp, email, or a shared Google Drive folder are all fine. Photographs and the logo are best sent as files or documents rather than as compressed images, so that quality is preserved.

If anything here is unclear, please just ask.
