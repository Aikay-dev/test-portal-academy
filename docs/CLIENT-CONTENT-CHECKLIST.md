## 1. Logo and branding — *Priority*

- [ ] Logo file — ideally **PNG with transparent background** or **SVG**. A high-resolution JPG works too.

- [ ] Confirm the brand colours are correct: **navy blue**, **green**, **gold** (taken from your flyer).

## 2. Team photos and details — *for the About page*

For **each** person to appear on the site:

- [ ] Full name
- [ ] Role / job title (e.g. Director, Lead Mathematics Tutor)
- [ ] Photograph — clear, well-lit, **head and shoulders**, facing the camera. A good phone photo is fine. Please avoid heavily filtered or group-cropped images.
- [ ] *Optional:* one or two lines about them (qualifications, years of experience, subjects taught)

**How many people?** Please confirm the number so the layout is designed correctly. Four to six works best visually.

---

## 3. Testimonials — *for the Testimonials page*

Your flyer has one quote. A dedicated testimonials page needs more to look credible.

- [ ] **4–6 genuine testimonials** from parents or students. For each:
  - The quote itself
  - Who it's from — first name is enough (e.g. *"Mrs. A., parent of Year 10 student"* or *"Year 11 Student"*)
  - Star rating out of 5, if you have one

> Please make sure you have permission to publish these, particularly where a student is under 18.
